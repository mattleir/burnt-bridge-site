About

Hi, I’m Coach Julia.

I’m an exercise physiologist, a competitive weightlifter, and a strength coach based in Vancouver, Washington.

Why the lab exists

Coaching people the right way.

I built Burnt Bridge Strength Lab out of my garage because I wanted to coach people the right way. Small roster. Real relationships. Programs built around the person standing in front of me, not copied from a spreadsheet.

Over ten years of coaching I’ve worked with complete beginners who’d never picked up a weight in their life. I’ve worked with competitive athletes who needed smarter programming. I’ve worked with busy professionals who have 30 minutes, three days a week, and need every minute to count.

And I’ve worked with a lot of people who used to train — who had a good routine going, then a job change or a move or a new kid happened — and they’re ready to find their way back to it. That one’s close to my heart.

You should leave every session feeling like you did something that mattered — and actually understand why you did it.

Strength training has a compounding effect. The stronger you get, the better you move. The better you move, the more you can do. I’ve watched it change people’s lives in ways that go way beyond the gym, and it doesn’t get old.

Credentials

A little background, if you want it.

  • Master’s, Exercise Physiology
  • CSCS
  • USA Weightlifting Level 2
  • Mobility Certification
  • Precision Nutrition Certification
  • 10 Years Coaching

I’ve spent my career working in clinical and coaching settings and have been coaching clients for ten years.

I tell you that not to impress you, but because I think you deserve a coach who has genuinely done the work to understand this stuff. There’s a lot of noise in the fitness industry. I try to cut through it.
