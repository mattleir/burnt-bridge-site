About Burnt Bridge

You don’t need more motivation.

You need a plan.

You’ve read the articles.
You’ve saved the workouts.
You’ve promised yourself, “I’ll start Monday.”

The problem isn’t motivation.
It’s not knowing where to begin—or how to keep going.

At Burnt Bridge, we make strength simple.
You’ll always know what you’re doing.
Why you’re doing it.
And exactly how you’re progressing.

Why we train

Build a body that works for your life.

Strength isn’t about spending hours in the gym.
It’s about everything outside of it.

Standing taller after years at a desk.
Keeping up with your kids.
Skiing without worrying about your knees.
Feeling confident carrying your own luggage.
Having the energy to say “yes” to adventure.

Your training should make life bigger.
Not busier.

Our approach

Three things every program follows.

Intelligent

Every exercise has a purpose.

Personal

Every program is built around your goals—not ours.

Sustainable

You don’t need six days a week. You need consistency. Two to three hours each week can completely change your health when you have the right plan.

Who we coach

Built for people who want to invest in themselves.

  • Professionals
  • Parents
  • Former athletes
  • Beginners
  • People returning after injury
  • People who simply want to feel capable again

If you’re willing to learn, we’ll meet you where you are.

What makes us different

A different question.

Most gyms ask, “How hard can we make this workout?” We ask, “How can we help you keep doing this five years from now?”

We don’t chase soreness.
We don’t chase trends.
We don’t ask you to prove yourself.

We build confidence through thoughtful coaching, smart programming, and measurable progress.
Because strength is earned—not rushed.

Meet Julia

Great coaching starts with listening.

Every client comes in with a different story. Different goals. Different injuries. Different fears.

My job isn’t to fit you into a program. It’s to build a program that fits you.

With a Master’s degree in Exercise Physiology, nearly two decades of coaching experience, and a lifelong love of strength training, I help people build bodies they’re proud to live in.

  • MS, Exercise Physiology
  • CSCS
  • USA Weightlifting Level 2
  • Mobility Certification
  • Precision Nutrition
