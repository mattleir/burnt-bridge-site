Questions

Before you book.

Do I need experience to start?
No. Most 1:1 and small group clients start at zero. The assessment tells us where to begin — not the other way around.
What’s the difference between remote and in-person?
Same programming philosophy, same data-driven adjustments. In-person adds live coaching and hands-on cueing.
How is load determined without a max test?
Movement quality and bar-speed data set your starting loads — safer and more accurate than a one-rep max on day one.
