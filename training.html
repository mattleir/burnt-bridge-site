Training & Pricing

Here’s how it works.

Every new client starts with a movement assessment. Not a generic plan — an understanding of you.

The approach

Programming that moves you forward every week.

Coach Julia goes through the fundamental patterns — squat, hinge, push, pull, carry — and gets a clear picture of how you move, where you’re strong, and where we need to build a foundation. This is where good programming starts.

From there, we train out of the Burnt Bridge Strength Lab in Vancouver. Sessions are an hour for most clients, and 30 minutes for people who need to work within a tighter schedule — both are legitimate options and both get results when the programming is dialed in.

The work is full body and progressive, meaning we’re always building on what came before. Your body adapts to stress placed on it consistently over time — that’s the science of how strength is built. We’re not doing random hard things. We’re following a plan.

Inside a session

What a session looks like.

  1. Warm-up & Mobility

    Tailored to what your body needs that day — not a fixed routine you run on autopilot.

  2. Compound Strength Work

    Built around the main movement patterns: squat, hinge, push, pull, carry.

  3. Accessory Work

    Targeting whatever you specifically need — weak links, imbalances, the things your goals depend on.

  4. Cooldown & Debrief

    What we did, why we did it, and what to notice before next time.

A note on getting back into it

You’re not starting from zero.

If you used to train and fell out of the habit, that’s one of Coach Julia’s favorite situations to work with.

Your body remembers more than you think. What you need is a re-entry point that meets you where you are now, not where you were three years ago. We build from there. Most people are surprised how quickly it comes back.

Why strength training specifically

The evidence is overwhelming.

People come to Burnt Bridge Strength Lab for a lot of different reasons — aesthetics, performance, injury prevention, general health. But the reason Coach Julia keeps coming back to strength training as the foundation for everyone is simple.

Regular strength training improves bone density, maintains muscle mass as you age, supports metabolic health, reduces the risk of injury, and improves quality of life in ways that cardio alone just doesn’t replicate. It also tends to make people feel more capable and confident — which isn’t a small thing.

If you’re only doing one thing for your health, it should probably be this.

Questions

Before you book.

I’ve never lifted before. Is that a problem?
Not at all — it’s one of the most common places people start. Your first session is a movement assessment, so we build the plan around how you actually move rather than assuming anything.
How often do I need to train?
Two or three days a week, full body. That’s enough to make real progress without taking over your calendar.
Are 30-minute sessions actually worth it?
Yes. Thirty focused, well-programmed minutes with a coach who knows your body is worth more than an hour of wandering around a gym wondering what to do next.
Do I need to do cardio too?
If you already have cardio you enjoy, keep it — plenty of clients handle their own conditioning and use these sessions purely for the strength side.
Where is the gym?
A private garage gym in Vancouver, WA. You’ll get the exact address when you book your consult.
